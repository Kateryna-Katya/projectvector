Emerald Expedition

About the Game

A survival adventure built around discovery.
The game combines exploration, environmental puzzles, survival mechanics, and narrative progression. Players travel through dangerous jungle regions while managing supplies, uncovering forgotten temples, and solving mysteries connected to an ancient civilization. It’s more Indiana Jones vibes than Resident Evil — we’re going for adventure and discovery, not trying to scare the crap out of you.

How to Play

  • Navigate Jungle Environments

    Cross rivers, climb cliffs, and travel through dense tropical terrain.

  • Discover Ancient Ruins

    Find hidden temples filled with mechanisms and forgotten artifacts.

  • Solve Environmental Puzzles

    Ancient locks, symbols, and traps block progression through ruins.

  • Expand Your Base Camp

    Upgrade equipment stations and prepare for deeper exploration.

Features

  • Different regions include waterfalls, caves, swamps, mountain ruins, and underground temples.

  • Rainstorms, fog, and changing daylight influence visibility and atmosphere.

  • The environment does most of the storytelling - you'll find these old temples and carvings that hint at what happened here.

  • Players unlock new traversal tools and survival upgrades over time.

  • Narrative clues gradually reveal the history of the lost city.
